MathQuest: free, fun maths lessons for kids

Step-by-step maths lessons with pictures, hands-on practice and real-life problems for children from Nursery to Year 6 (ages 3 to 11). Pip the owl explains one small step at a time, then children try it themselves. Lessons follow the England National Curriculum for Key Stage 1 and Key Stage 2. Free, with no sign-up.

Maths topics

  • Numbers 0–10 (Nursery): spot, count and play with numbers up to 10, read aloud.
  • Place Value: counting, tens and ones, comparing, rounding and estimating.
  • Addition: number bonds, counting on, number lines and column addition with carrying.
  • Subtraction: taking away, finding the difference and column subtraction with exchanging.
  • Multiplication: equal groups, arrays and times tables.
  • Division: sharing fairly, grouping and bus-stop division.
  • Fractions: halves, quarters and parts of a whole.
  • Decimals & Percentages (from Year 4): tenths, hundredths and per cent.
  • Negative Numbers (from Year 3): below zero with cold days, lifts and debts.
  • Money: coins, shopping, change and saving in pounds, euros or dollars.
  • Time & Clocks: read clocks, digital and 24-hour time, durations and timetables.
  • Measures: length, mass and capacity.
